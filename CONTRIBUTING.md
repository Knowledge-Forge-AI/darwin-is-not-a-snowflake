# Contributing to Darwin Is Not a Snowflake

Darwin Is Not a Snowflake accepts contributions only under the project contribution terms in [CLA.md](CLA.md).

Contributors retain copyright in their contributions. By submitting a contribution, the contributor grants Samuel Leighton Lair, as Darwin Is Not a Snowflake project steward, broad copyright and patent rights to use, modify, distribute, sublicense, and relicense the contribution as part of Darwin Is Not a Snowflake, including under AGPL-3.0-or-later and commercial licenses.

Contributions must be original work or submitted with authority from the copyright owner. Do not submit code, documentation, generated output, or other content unless you have the right to provide it under these terms.

Every outside contributor must agree to [CLA.md](CLA.md). Every pull request must include this explicit acknowledgment in the pull request body:

```text
I agree to the Darwin Is Not a Snowflake contribution terms in CLA.md.
```

A `Signed-off-by` line may be included, but it does not replace the explicit pull request acknowledgment. Contributions without that acknowledgment cannot be accepted.

Pull requests should also confirm that the contributor has authority to submit the work under `CLA.md`.

Questions about commercial licensing or contribution terms can be sent to sam.lair@knowledge-forge.ai.

Identify third-party material and preserve its license, copyright, attribution and notice requirements. Third-party rights are not replaced by this repository's licensing model. Follow the repository's technical scope and verification requirements.
